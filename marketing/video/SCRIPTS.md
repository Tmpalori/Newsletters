# Subscribe video ads: 5 towns, 2 each

Two ads per town. **Video 1** is Halloween week and runs **Sun Oct 25 to Sat Oct 31**. **Video 2** is evergreen and runs **from Sun Nov 1 for as long as you like**: it has no dates, and it's built on what your readers said they want, with live music first.

Every event in Video 1 was checked on its own page on Oct 23.

**Filming:** same as your Victoria ad: vertical, phone at eye level, talk like you're telling a friend. Each script is about 40-46 words, which is 13-16 seconds. **End on the newsletter's name** ("...just click the link to get The Vic 361"): the end card comes up right as you say it. Do two takes of each. You can film all ten in one go and drop them in the Town Newsletters folder; I'll sort out which is which.

I add the corner badge, the event cards, the captions and the end card, in the style of your Victoria ad.
